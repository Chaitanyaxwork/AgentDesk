## Repository-derived setup

- Required public Supabase configuration: `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` (or legacy `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`). Server-side elevated key: `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY` (or legacy `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`). The server key bypasses RLS and must never be public.

- `GEMINI_BILLING_DISABLED=true` and `GEMINI_UNPAID_DATA_APPROVED=true` are operator assertions. They do not query Google billing or record an approval outside the environment.
- The exact migrations are in `AGENTDESK-SETUP.md` and `AGENTDESK-STAGING-CHECKLIST.md`: `supabase/schema.sql`, then `20261014_ai_usage_and_function_grants.sql`, `20261015_billing_and_tenant_hardening.sql`, `20261016_atomic_workspace_provisioning.sql`, and `20261017_chat_idempotency.sql`.
